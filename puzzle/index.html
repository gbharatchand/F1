How to Play

🧩 Each level shows one picture cut into jigsaw pieces.
👉 Drag every piece from the right onto its matching spot on the board.
🖼️ When all the slices join up, the full picture appears!
⭐ Level 1 has 4 pieces. Every level adds more.
